To use your own video:
1. Add your video file to this assets folder (e.g., hero-video.mp4)
2. Update the video source in HeroSection.tsx to import and use your local video
3. Recommended video specs:
   - Format: MP4 (H.264)
   - Resolution: 1920x1080 or higher
   - Duration: 10-30 seconds (looping)
   - File size: < 10MB for optimal performance

Example import:
import heroVideo from "@/assets/hero-video.mp4";

Then use in video tag:
<source src={heroVideo} type="video/mp4" />
